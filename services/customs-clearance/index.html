Common questions

All FAQs
How fast can you clear my shipment?

If your documents reach us by [10:00], we file the declaration the same working day. Release time then depends on whether customs selects your shipment for inspection; we attend the inspection and keep you updated at every step.

What documents do you need from me?

The commercial invoice, packing list and bill of lading (or air waybill). For exports, add the booking confirmation. If your goods need a permit or a certificate of origin, we’ll tell you exactly which one.

What happens if my shipment is held for inspection?

Our officer attends the inspection with the customs inspector, answers questions on the spot and settles any duties so your container is released as quickly as possible. You’ll hear from us the moment it’s selected.

Do you manage our CDC master list?

Yes. We track every duty-free item on your master list against each import declaration, and warn you before any item’s balance runs low.
