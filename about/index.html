Making customs clearance simple for Cambodia’s factories

Customs was meant to move goods, not hold them. Yet containers still sit at the port, not because the rules are unclear, but because paperwork, checks and handovers fall between too many hands.

?

Why we exist

  • Coverage

    10 customs ports

    From Sihanoukville Port to the Bavet border, we clear shipments at seaports, river ports, dry ports and Techo International Airport, so one team follows your goods wherever they land.

  • Speed

    Same working day

    That’s when your declaration is filed through the National Single Window, when your documents reach us by [10:00]. Every hour a container waits is an hour a production line waits with it.

  • Accuracy

    Within hours

    That’s how quickly we check every description, tariff code, value and weight. Most holds start with a small mistake on paper, so we catch it before customs ever sees it.

  • Clarity

    1 invoice per shipment

    In Khmer and English, tied to the same declaration, with duties and port charges shown at cost alongside the official receipts. You know the price before you ship, with no surprise charges after.

Customs should be handled, not chased

Grateful Solutions exists to close the gap between the port and your production line, through careful document checks, same-day declarations and one team from the port to your factory gate.
