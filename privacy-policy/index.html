Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15.10.2026 Published: 15.10.2026

At Grateful Solutions, we handle your shipment documents and business details every day. This policy explains what information we collect, why we need it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

1. About this policy

Grateful Solutions (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. ("Grateful Solutions", "we", "us" or "our") is a customs broker based in Phnom Penh. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices you have.

It applies to information we receive through this website, by phone, Telegram, email or in person, and while we provide customs clearance, permit, certificate of origin, CDC master list and trucking services.

We handle personal information in line with the applicable laws of the Kingdom of Cambodia. By using our website or services, you acknowledge that your information will be handled as described here.

2. Information we collect

We only collect information that we reasonably need to quote for, carry out and administer our services.

A. Contact and business details

  • Name, job title and the company you represent;
  • Phone number, Telegram account, email address and business address;
  • Company registration details, VAT (TIN) number and other information needed to file declarations in your company's name.

B. Shipment and customs documents

To clear your goods we receive documents that may contain personal information about you or others, including:

  • Commercial invoices, packing lists, bills of lading and air waybills;
  • Booking confirmations, permits, licences and certificates of origin;
  • CDC master lists, duty-free quotas and import and export records;
  • Names and contact details of suppliers, buyers, consignees and shipping agents;
  • Delivery addresses, and driver and vehicle details for trucking.

C. Payment and billing information

  • Quotes, tax invoices, disbursements and debit notes;
  • Payment references and bank details needed to receive or refund payments.

D. Communications

  • Messages, calls and emails you exchange with our team;
  • Information you send through forms on this website, such as quote requests or newsletter sign-ups.

E. Website and technical information

When you visit this website, your browser automatically shares technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device and the pages you request. This is used to deliver the website and keep it secure.

3. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Answer enquiries and prepare quotes;
  • Check documents and prepare, file and follow up customs declarations;
  • Apply for permits and certificates of origin on your behalf;
  • Maintain and balance your CDC master list;
  • Arrange inspections, release and delivery of your goods;
  • Issue invoices, collect payments and keep accounting records;
  • Keep you updated on the status of your shipments;
  • Send our customs update newsletter if you have subscribed;
  • Improve our website and services, and protect them from misuse;
  • Meet our legal, tax, customs and regulatory obligations.

We will not use your information for a purpose unrelated to why it was collected unless you agree or the law permits it.

4. Sharing with authorities and partners

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Because customs clearance involves government agencies and logistics partners, we share information where it is needed to move your goods or required by law, including with:

  • The General Department of Customs and Excise of Cambodia (GDCE) and other government ministries and agencies that issue permits, licences or certificates;
  • Cambodia's National Single Window and related electronic filing systems;
  • Port, dry port and airport operators, shipping lines, airlines and freight forwarders;
  • Trucking companies and drivers delivering your containers;
  • Banks and payment providers;
  • Our accountants, auditors and legal advisers;
  • IT, email and cloud service providers that help us run our business;
  • Anyone else you ask us to share information with, or where the law requires it.

5. Transfers outside Cambodia

Some of the parties involved in your shipment, such as overseas suppliers, buyers and shipping lines, and some of our service providers, may be located outside Cambodia. Information sent to them may be stored and processed in other countries. When this happens, we share only what is needed for the shipment or service.

6. Cookies and third-party services

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. To load quickly, it uses fonts, scripts and libraries delivered from third-party content delivery networks, which receive your IP address and basic browser information when your browser requests those files.

If we add analytics or other tools that use cookies in the future, we will update this policy before doing so. You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings.

7. Newsletter and marketing

If you subscribe to The Grateful Customs Update, we use your name and email address to send you updates on customs rules, tariffs, the National Single Window and certificates of origin.

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any newsletter or by contacting us. We will still send you messages that are needed for ongoing shipments or services.

8. Information about other people

Shipping documents often include details of other people, such as your suppliers, buyers, staff or drivers. When you send us this information, please make sure you are allowed to share it with us for the purpose of clearing and delivering your goods.

9. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information and shipment documents against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. Access is limited to staff and partners who need it to handle your shipment.

No method of sending or storing information over the internet is completely secure. Please use the contact details below if you need to send especially sensitive documents and want to agree a secure method first.

10. How long we keep information

We keep personal information for as long as we need it to provide our services and for as long as customs, tax, accounting and other laws require us to keep records of declarations and transactions. When it is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

11. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or out of date;
  • Delete information we no longer need;
  • Stop sending you marketing messages.

Some information must be kept to meet legal or customs record-keeping obligations, so we may not be able to delete everything. We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, the law or our practices change. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated shown at the top.
