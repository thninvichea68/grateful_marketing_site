Pricing & Trust

How much does customs clearance cost?

It depends on the port, container size and type of goods. Import clearance and export clearance per shipment. Send us your information, and we’ll give you an exact quote before you ship.

Are you a licensed customs broker?

Yes. Grateful Solutions (Cambodia) Co., Ltd. is a licensed customs broker registered with the General Department of Customs and Excise of Cambodia (GDCE). Our VAT number is K008-902404873.

What’s included in your price?

Document checks, the customs declaration, attending inspection, and release at the port. Duties, taxes and port charges are paid at cost and shown separately on your disbursement, with the official receipts.

Are there any hidden charges?

No. Every cost appears on your quote first, and every shipment gets one tax invoice, one disbursement and one debit note, all tied to the same declaration, in Khmer and English.

Speed & Process

How fast can you clear my shipment?

If your documents reach us by [10:00], we file the declaration the same working day. Release time then depends on whether customs selects your shipment for inspection; we attend the inspection and keep you updated at every step.

What documents do you need from me?

The commercial invoice, packing list and bill of lading (or air waybill). For exports, add the booking confirmation. If your goods need a permit or a certificate of origin, we’ll tell you exactly which one.

Which ports do you cover?

Ten customs ports, including Sihanoukville Port, Phnom Penh Port, the O’Lair, Tecsrun and Royal Railway dry ports, Techo International Airport and the Bavet border.

Can you deliver the container to my factory?

Yes. Once it’s released, we truck your 20' or 40' container straight from the port to your factory gate.

What happens if my shipment is held for inspection?

Our officer attends the inspection with the customs inspector, answers questions on the spot and settles any duties so your container is released as quickly as possible. You’ll hear from us the moment it’s selected.

Documents & Support

Can you prepare certificates of origin for exports?

Yes. We handle Form A, Form D and GSP REX for shipments to the US, Europe, Japan and ASEAN countries.

Do you manage our CDC master list?

Yes. We track every duty-free item on your master list against each import declaration, and warn you before any item’s balance runs low.

Do you use the National Single Window?

Yes. We file declarations and permit applications through Cambodia’s National Single Window (NSW), so your shipment is processed electronically from the start.

Can I talk to a real person?

Always. Call us on +855 98 484 414 or email [email], [days], [hours]. You’ll speak to the officer who knows your shipment.

Do you work with new clients and small shipments?

Yes. We clear everything from a single LCL shipment to regular full-container loads. Send us your first shipment’s documents and we’ll guide you through it.
